    03Power

    Power is the gap. Pausing closes it.

    The building's own service is a fraction of what AI racks draw, so it needs more from the grid. Utilities can connect load sooner when it eases off during the grid's tightest hours. Here is exactly what that would have meant in 2025, hour by hour, from ERCOT's own load data.

    05Evidence

    Every number shows its work.

    Click any underlined number to see its source, when it was retrieved and how it was calculated. Each figure carries one of five labels.

    Measured

    Read from our own hardware: machine watts and job progress.

    Sourced

    From a named dataset with a date: CoStar records, FEMA flood zones, ERCOT load.

    Derived by a documented method: distances, service size, savings, what the agent sees in an image.

    Scenario or replay: simulated machines when no hardware is connected.
